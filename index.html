Word Weeds

How to play

  • Claim words by dragging across letters that touch, in any direction, diagonals included.
  • Move letters by tapping two tiles to swap them, or by dragging a letter into an empty space.
  • Survival: new letters keep sprouting. When the grid fills, a red line runs around the board like a stopwatch. Clear a word before it gets back to the top.
  • Scoring: a word scores its length squared (a 5-letter word is 25 points), and points grow the longer you survive.
  • Hints: now and then the tiles of a word shimmer in order. You can turn hints off in Settings.

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same letters each day. Play as often as you like: your best score of the day counts.

A new puzzle starts every day at midnight Pacific Standard Time (8:00 UTC), at the same moment worldwide. The game shows when that is in your time zone.

Leaderboards

On iPhone and iPad, Word Weeds can post your Survival and Daily Challenge scores to Game Center leaderboards. It's optional: turn off Game Center leaderboards in the game's Settings, or don't sign in to Game Center, and nothing is shared. Your own high scores are always kept on your device.
